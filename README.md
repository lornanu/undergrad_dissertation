# Undergraduate Dissertation Data
data analysis in R for my undergraduate Psychology dissertation project.

The study investigated whether fear of negative evaluation, self-objectification, enjoyment of sexualisation, and social media use could predict self-sexualisation in women. 
Data was collected using a survey containing 5 empirically validated scales.

## Data cleanup
507 participants were recruited for this study. Participants who had completed less than 75% of the survey, and those who did not identify as a woman were removed, leaving a total of 372 participants (Mean= 42.6, SD= 16.0).

## Variables
- Self-objectification Scale (SOBBS; Lindner and Tantleff-Dunn, 2017)
- Active social media engagement (PAUM; Gerson et al., 2017)
- Passive social media engagement (PAUM; Gerson et al., 2017)
- Enjoyment of sexualisation (ESS; Liss et al., 2011)
- Fear of negative evaluation (BFNE-S; Rodebaugh, 2004)
DV:
-Self-sexualisation (SBBQ-W; Smolak et al., 2014)


## Summary of main findings
Results of a multiple regression found that passive social media use, active social media use, and enjoyment of sexualisation were found to predict self-sexualisation, while self-objectification and fear of negative evaluation were not significant predictors.
